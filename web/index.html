AM2R 3DS SD Card Maker

Turns your AM2R 1.1 zip into a ready-to-copy SD card folder for the AM2R 3DS port, updated to the Community Updates . It all runs in this browser: your files aren't uploaded anywhere.

1 Your AM2R 1.1 zip

2 The Community Updates patch

3 Make the SD card zip

It takes a few minutes and makes a zip of about 550 MB. Keep this tab open until it's done.

Copy it to the SD card

    
    
      
  1. Put your 3DS's SD card in your computer (or connect over FTP).
    2. 
      
  2. Extract the zip to the root of the SD card, so the files end up in
        sdmc:/3ds/am2r/ (the card's 3ds folder, then am2r).
    3. 
      
  3. Updating an older copy? Let it replace the files, but keep your own config.ini if you changed
        the controls. Your saves aren't in the zip, so they stay.
    4. 
    


    
Install the game (am2r.cia)

    

      
      
    
        
  1. On the 3DS, open FBI, choose Remote Install, then
          Scan QR Code, and scan this code. FBI downloads and installs am2r.cia.
    2. 
        
  2. No camera or no Wi-Fi? Download it from , copy it to the
          SD card, and install it with FBI from there.
    3. 
        
  3. Start AM2R from the HOME Menu.
    4.
